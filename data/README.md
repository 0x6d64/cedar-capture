# data/

Git-ignored. Put your recordings here (desk and car recordings, logging sheets). They are personal data: do not commit
them, and do not let agents read them (`.claude/settings.json` denies it).

Suggested layout:

```
data/recordings/2026-11-20-desk.wav        16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV
data/recordings/2026-11-20-desk.facts.txt  your logging sheet (docs/testing/test-protocol.md section 4)
data/transcripts/                           transcripts produced by the engines you test
```
